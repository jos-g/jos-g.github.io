An interdisciplinary teaching and outreach project exploring the relationship between Physics and Music, developed at the Universidade do Estado do Rio de Janeiro (UERJ) under the supervision of Maria Lúcia Netto Grillo.

The project studied the physical characteristics of sound, its propagation, and its neural processing, and included science-outreach talks and didactic recitals for the public.

The work led to a case study on the bassoon, published as {% cite grillo2023fisicamusicafagote %}.
